# Official module list

The official MindCare module list, saved verbatim. Planning and status reports are
organised by these modules. Per-bullet status lives in
[status/module-tracker.md](status/module-tracker.md).

6.1 User Onboarding and Profile Module
- Users will register by providing basic information such as name, age,
  gender, and contact details, with an optional input for mental health
  history.
- A profile photo can be uploaded, and the user will have the ability to
  view and edit their profile information at any time.
- User will be able to select which of his information is public and
  which is private.
6.2 Psychologist Onboarding Module
- Psychologists will register by providing personal and professional
  information, including certifications, medical background, and
  specialization areas.
- Psychologists will upload their credentials and licenses for
  verification, ensuring compliance with health standards.
- Psychologists will be able to set their availability, manage session
  bookings, and integrate their preferred video conferencing tools
  (e.g., Zoom, Google Meet).
6.3 Admin Panel
- Admins will verify psychologist credentials and approve or reject
  their registration requests.
- Admins will monitor platform activities and ensure users and
  psychologists follow platform policies.
- Admins will moderate community content and manage reports or
  complaints submitted by users.
6.4 Patient Profiling Module
- Psychologists will maintain patient therapy notes and observations
  after each session.
- Psychologists will track patient progress and changes in mental health
  condition over time.
- Users will be able to view summarized insights of their mental health
  progress.
6.5 Sensor Monitoring and Health Tracking Module
- The app will track users' breathing rate, heart rate and sweating
  through integrated sensors.
- This data will be used to monitor users' mental and physical states in
  real-time and help personalize exercise recommendations.
- Notifications and reminders will be provided to users based on their
  sensor data, such as sleep improvement tips or activity-based
  suggestions.
6.6 AI Recommendation Integrated with Therapy Sessions
- AI will analyze the user's mental health data and sensor data to
  recommend personalized exercises, sleeping schedule and diet plan.
- The AI will learn from psychologist feedback by approval or updation
  and progress to continuously refine its recommendations.
- The AI recommendations will get approved or updated by the
  psychologist.
6.7 AI Chat Assistance
- The AI-powered chatbot will be available 24/7 to provide immediate
  support to users, answering common mental health-related questions.
- It will suggest therapy or wellness options based on the user's mental
  health concerns and preferences.
- The chatbot will help users with initial triage, offering basic coping
  strategies or directing them to appropriate resources or
  psychologists.
6.8 Community Support Module
- Users will be able to join or create communities where they can share
  personal experiences, struggles, and success stories related to
  mental health.
- Each community will provide a space to interact, offer advice,
  volunteer, and organize real-world activities such as dinners, walks,
  or hikes to reduce loneliness and build connections.
- These interactions will foster emotional support, a sense of
  belonging, and mutual understanding among users.
6.9 Lifestyle and Wellness Module
- Content related to improving overall well-being, such as diet plans,
  exercise routines, and stress-reduction techniques.
- Personalized wellness suggestions based on mental health needs and
  sensor data, like sleep or physical activity tracking.
- Resources on healthy lifestyle habits, including mindfulness, yoga,
  nutrition, and relaxation techniques.
6.10 Motivation Corner
- Duas (audio + text)
- Quotes
- Surahs (audio + text)
6.11 Payment Module
- Users can view session fees, total sessions taken, and the total
  amount paid through their profile.
- Multiple secure payment methods (e.g., credit/debit card, mobile
  wallets) integrated into the platform.
- Handles invoicing, receipts, and session booking, ensuring
  transparency for both users and psychologists.
6.12 NGO Onboarding & Emergency SOS Module
- A dedicated SOS button accessible from all screens, allowing users in
  distress to instantly trigger an emergency support flow.
- The system provides helplines, nearby hospitals, and notifies
  pre-configured emergency contacts while attempting real-time
  connection with an available psychologist.
- The AI chatbot detects distress signals and proactively activates SOS
  support; all emergency activities are logged for admin monitoring and
  follow-up.
6.13 Engagement & Rewards System
- Users earn points, badges, and maintain streaks by completing
  wellness activities, therapy sessions, and daily check-ins.
- A progression system motivates users through levels and milestones.
- Rewards such as discounts, premium content, and session benefits,
  with optional leaderboards.
6.14 Automated Report Generation
- Weekly and monthly reports summarizing user activities, mood patterns,
  and therapy progress for psychologist review.
- Reports include visual insights such as trends, behavioral changes,
  and key indicators from sensor data and user interactions.
- Psychologists use these reports to monitor progress, adjust treatment
  plans, and provide data-driven care.
